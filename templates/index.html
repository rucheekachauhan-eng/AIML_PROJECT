    How The Machine Learning System Works

    Four-stage pipeline from raw demographic data to calibrated cost tier prediction

    01

    Patient Input & Validation

    Captures continuous and categorical indicators: age, BMI, smoker status, children, and region, validated against clinical boundaries.

    02

    ColumnTransformer Pipeline

    Standardizes numerical variables with StandardScaler and one-hot encodes categoricals via OneHotEncoder to prevent leakage.

    03

    Decision Tree Classification

    Evaluates non-linear feature interactions (such as the exponential risk of high BMI combined with smoking) at 90.7% accuracy.

    04

    Tier Mapping & Logging

    Stratifies the output into Low, Medium, or High dollar brackets, generates risk insights, and persists records in SQLite for analysis.
